# Task 3 — Ground truth for legal summary

The summary must convey these key points AND correctly define the listed key terms in lay language.

Key points (7):
1. Each side promises to defend/pay the other when third parties sue them because of the first side's mistake, breach, bad faith, or IP infringement.
2. The IP-infringement promise does NOT apply if the customer modified the deliverable, combined it with outside stuff that caused the problem, or used it beyond what the contract allows.
3. The party being defended must give prompt written notice; late notice only excuses the other side if it caused real harm.
4. The defending party runs the lawsuit and settlement, but can't settle in a way that admits fault or imposes non-money obligations without consent.
5. Neither side owes "indirect" damages (lost profits, lost data, etc.) — with exceptions listed below.
6. Exceptions to the damages cap: indemnity obligations, confidentiality breaches, gross negligence/willful misconduct, and IP infringement.
7. Cap on ordinary damages = the greater of (a) fees paid in the last 12 months, or (b) $100,000.

Key terms to define (8):
- Indemnify / hold harmless
- Third-party claim
- Gross negligence vs. willful misconduct
- Intellectual property infringement / misappropriation
- Consequential / incidental / punitive damages
- Cap on liability
- Sole control of the defense
- Materially prejudiced (late notice)

Scoring:
- 12+ of 15 items covered correctly: success
- 7-11 of 15 covered: partial
- 0-6: fail
